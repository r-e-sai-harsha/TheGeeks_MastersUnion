Chaya छाया · system architecture

Two data streams in, one calibrated virtual patient, 2-hour spike prediction and what-if simulation out
1 · Data streams

Static / historical: EHR

FHIR R4 Bundle per patient

  • Demographics, city, diet
  • Diagnoses (SNOMED): T2D, HTN, NAFLD
  • Labs (LOINC): HbA1c, FPG, lipids, eGFR
  • Medications: metformin, glimepiride, DPP-4i, SGLT2i, insulin
  • Genetics: TCF7L2 rs7903146

Dynamic / real-time: wearables

  • CGM glucose, every 5 min
  • Steps, heart rate
  • Nightly HRV (RMSSD), sleep stages
  • Meal log (Indian dishes, carbs, GI)
  • Medication log

Synthetic cohort

150 Indian patients × 14 days · 604,800 sensor rows · physiology-driven generator. No real patient data.

2 · Ingest & harmonise

FHIR parser

LOINC / SNOMED codes → patient profile vector

Stream alignment

5-min grid · sensor gaps kept as gaps · meal and dose events placed on the timeline

Model inputs

Minute-level carbs, GI-dependent absorption, steps, sleep-debt and missed-dose modifiers

3 · Digital twin core

Per-patient physiological model

gut → plasma glucose ⇄ insulin action
extended Bergman minimal model

1

EHR prior. BMI, waist, HbA1c, duration, TCF7L2, drugs → starting physiology

2

Calibrate. MAP fit to 7 days of CGM: insulin sensitivity, β-cell response, basal glucose, meal bioavailability, dawn effect

3

Observe. Runs beside live CGM; corrects its state and detects unlogged meals

4

Habit model. Learns when and how much this patient usually eats

5

Forecast & what-if. Simulates the next 2–4 h under routine or a doctor's scenario

↻ re-calibrates as new data arrives

Patient nudge (next)

“Walk 15 min after lunch today”

Evaluation protocol

Patient-level split 105 train / 45 held-out. Temporal split: days 1–7 calibrate, days 8–14 predict. Threshold chosen on training folds only.

Privacy by design

Synthetic data only (DPDP Act 2023 / HIPAA sandbox rules). FHIR-native so it can plug into ABDM-consented records later.
