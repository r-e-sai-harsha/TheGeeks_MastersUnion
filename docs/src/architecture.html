Chaya छाया · system architecture

Two data streams in, one calibrated virtual patient; 2-hour spike and low-glucose warnings, what-if simulation and a patient app out. Patient logs flow back in.
1 · Data streams

Static / historical: EHR

FHIR R4 Bundle per patient

  • Demographics, city, diet
  • Diagnoses (SNOMED): T2D, HTN, NAFLD
  • Labs (LOINC): HbA1c, FPG, lipids, eGFR
  • Medications: metformin, glimepiride, DPP-4i, SGLT2i, insulin
  • Genetics: TCF7L2 rs7903146

Dynamic / real-time: wearables

  • CGM glucose, every 5 min
  • Steps, heart rate
  • Nightly HRV (RMSSD), sleep stages
  • Meal and dose logs, now from the patient app

Synthetic cohort

150 Indian patients × 14 days · 604,800 sensor rows · physiology-driven generator. No real patient data.

2 · Ingest & harmonise

FHIR parser

LOINC / SNOMED codes → patient profile vector

Stream alignment

5-min grid · sensor gaps kept as gaps · meal and dose events placed on the timeline

Model inputs

Minute-level carbs, GI-dependent absorption, steps, sleep-debt and missed-dose modifiers

3 · Digital twin core

Per-patient physiological model

gut → plasma glucose ⇄ insulin action
extended Bergman minimal model

1

EHR prior. BMI, waist, HbA1c, duration, TCF7L2, drugs → starting physiology

2

Calibrate. MAP fit to 7 days of CGM: insulin sensitivity, β-cell response, basal glucose, meal bioavailability, dawn effect

3

Observe. Runs beside live CGM; corrects its state and detects unlogged meals

4

Habit model. Learns when and how much this patient usually eats

5

Forecast & what-if. Simulates the next 2–4 h under routine or a doctor's scenario

↻ re-calibrates as new data arrives

Evaluation protocol

Patient-level split 105 train / 45 held-out. Temporal split: days 1–7 calibrate, days 8–14 predict. Threshold chosen on training folds only.

Privacy by design

Synthetic data only (DPDP Act 2023 / HIPAA sandbox rules). FHIR-native so it can plug into ABDM-consented records later.

Stack

Python · NumPy/Numba · SciPy · LightGBM · FastAPI (local pipeline) · Vercel functions (Mumbai) · Supabase Postgres · vanilla JS + D3

From a warning to an action the patient sees

What happens after the twin raises a warning, where each step is stored, and how the live prototype is deployed
The closed care loop
1 · warn

Twin + model

Spike likely or Low likely, 2 h ahead, with three reasons. Threshold set by the clinic (2.6–4.6 warnings per patient a day).

2 · review

Clinician

Reads the stamp and reasons; marks the warning reviewed.

alert_reviews

3 · escalate

Owner + due time

Care coordinator, doctor on call or educator. Prefilled WhatsApp or a call log; overdue moves to the doctor on call.

escalations

4 · advise

Rx pad

Test a meal, walk, sleep or missed dose on the twin, then save and send.

case_notes

↓ then on the patient's phone, and back to the clinic
5 · reach

Patient app

Advice arrives in the doctor's hand with the peak it should prevent. A toast in the clinic links straight to it.

6 · read

Read receipt

Patient taps Got it; the clinic sees when.

case_notes.ack_at

7 · log

Meals and doses

Logged in two taps; land on the clinic's day trace and the twin's inputs.

patient_logs

8 · measure

Clinic outcomes

Time in range, low safety, alert load and the funnel warnings → reviewed → escalated → contacted → advice → seen.

What is new in this version

Low-glucose warnings

Same twin, read downwards; shown first on the patient's phone with the 15 g sugar rule.

Escalation and outcomes

Every warning has an owner; the buyer sees whether warnings became action.

Bilingual patient app

English and Hindi, dose reminders, logging, advice read receipts, its own 11-step tour.

Clinic workspace

Pinned panes, ℞ Advise / Prescribe dock, a health record that separates point-in-time values from CGM trends, info tooltips, pulsing entry buttons, 25 data sources documented.

Offline fallback

If the API is unreachable after a cold-start wait and one retry, the app says Offline demo and runs from bundled data; changes stay in the browser.

Offline compute

run_all.sh: data → twins → LightGBM → metrics → web data. build_outcomes.js: low flags, threshold curve, daily time in range, using the browser's own simulator.
